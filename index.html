The White Lotus

A private archive for people who suspect the official account is the shortest version of the story.

It runs in three directions. The institutions that prefer not to be studied. The older material those institutions were built on. And the practices that both were meant to keep out of ordinary hands.

Everything is sourced. Where a claim can be documented, the document goes up with it. Where it can't, it is marked as exactly that.

The Old Texts

Enoch, the Tree of Life, the Hermetic corpus, alchemy. The material every one of those orders was assembled on top of, read directly instead of through the people who claimed it.
